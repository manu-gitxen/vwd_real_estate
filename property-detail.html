Ultra-Luxury Signature Beachfront Villa

Frond M, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai
AED 34,500,000 AED 4,058 / sq.ft
Get Directions

Description

VWD Real Estate LLC is privileged to present this one-of-a-kind ultra-luxury Signature Beachfront Villa situated on the most prestigious frond of Palm Jumeirah. Crafted with uncompromising attention to architectural perfection, this bespoke private estate commands sweeping, uninterrupted vistas across the Arabian Gulf, Dubai Marina skyline, and the iconic Ain Dubai. Double-height ceilings and motorized Schuco glass facades bathe the grand salon in natural sunlight, while bookmatched Italian Calacatta marble flooring extends outwards to the private infinity pool deck. Culinary enthusiasts will appreciate the dual kitchen concept: a fully equipped European show kitchen featuring bespoke Poliform joinery and integrated Miele appliances, complemented by a discreet chef's prep kitchen. Upstairs, five generous en-suite sanctuaries include a palatial master wing with a private sunset terrace, boutique dressing salon, and a spa-inspired bathroom clad in marble with freestanding soaking tub.

Editorial Description

VWD Real Estate LLC is privileged to present this one-of-a-kind ultra-luxury Signature Beachfront Villa situated on the most prestigious frond of Palm Jumeirah. Crafted with uncompromising attention to architectural perfection, this bespoke private estate commands sweeping, uninterrupted vistas across the Arabian Gulf, Dubai Marina skyline, and the iconic Ain Dubai.

Spanning an expansive 8,500 square feet of built-up area on an 11,200 square-foot beachfront plot, the residence seamlessly blends minimalist contemporary elegance with organic warmth. Double-height ceilings and motorized floor-to-ceiling Schuco glass facades bathe the grand salon in natural sunlight, while bookmatched Italian Calacatta marble flooring extends outwards to the private infinity pool deck.

Culinary enthusiasts will appreciate the dual kitchen concept: a fully equipped European show kitchen featuring bespoke Poliform joinery and integrated Miele appliances, complemented by a discreet chef's prep kitchen. Upstairs, five generous en-suite sanctuaries include a palatial master wing with a private sunset terrace, boutique dressing salon, and a spa-inspired bathroom clad in marble with freestanding soaking tub.
